# Home

**Home** (`/`) is the event at a glance: our standing, the next match with a countdown and who we play with and
against, scouting coverage, recent results, and **Your assignments** (the next 3 open assignments, with "See all N"
linking to My Reports; see [assignment cards](../scouting/assignment-cards.md)). It refreshes every 2 minutes. On phones it is one screen with no scrolling.
