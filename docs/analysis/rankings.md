# Power Rankings and Match Rankings

**Match Rankings** (`/matchrankings`): after a match the group orders the robots best to worst
(`scouting_match_rankings.ranked_team_keys`, an ordered array). **Power Rankings** (`/powerrankings`) combines scouting
power with those rankings and each scout's per-match power ranking (see
[Match Scouting](../scouting/match-scouting.md#sections)), with head-to-head comparisons and star plots. Team pickers are
searchable popups, and a typed-in robot can be removed with its x button.
